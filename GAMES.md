# The Extra Mile — Game Designs (for approval)

Eleven games, all themed to **The Extra Mile** (customer service, the open road)
and the **Hot Wheels / racing** motif, in 7-Eleven orange / green / red. Every
game is an **original build** — no copies of Pac-Man, Galaga, Frogger, Space
Invaders, or official Hot Wheels art. Where a mechanic is a classic, we name our
own version below.

Scoring is uniform: play a round → a score is posted to the live leaderboard.
The one **head-to-head** game is the racer (Thu & Fri).

Please review and reply **approve** (or mark up any game). These designs drive
Phases 1–4; the app foundation, day-switching, leaderboard, and scoring are
already built around them.

---

## Monday

### 1. License Plate Challenge — `license-plate`
Plates from different states scroll by on a highway. Tap the state the prompt
asks for ("Find **Texas**") before it drives off-screen. Faster taps and streaks
score more; a wrong tap breaks the streak. 60-second round.
*Extra Mile tie-in:* going the distance on a road trip. **Content needed:** none
(state list built in).

### 2. Road Trivia — `road-trivia`
Multiple-choice questions on customer service and road-trip/7-Eleven fun facts.
Faster correct answers score higher; 10 questions a round.
**Content needed:** a question set. I'll draft ~25 questions (customer-service +
road-trip themed) for your review unless you supply them.

## Tuesday

### 3. Extra Mile Bingo — `extra-mile-bingo`
A 5×5 road-trip bingo card (road signs, mile markers, service moments). Items
are "called" on a timer; tap them as they appear. Completed lines score;
blackout is the jackpot. Single round, ~2–3 min.
**Content needed:** none (I'll theme the 24 squares; you can swap any).

### 4. Roadside Detour — `roadside-detour` (mini escape room)
A short "your route is blocked — solve it to get back on the road" puzzle: 3–4
linked clues (a code lock, a matching puzzle, a riddle). Score = time left when
solved. One playthrough.
**Content needed:** I'll draft the puzzle; you approve the theme/answers.

## Wednesday (arcade set)

### 5. Snack Run — `muncher` (maze-muncher, original)
Steer a cart through a store-aisle maze, grab all the Slurpee cups, avoid the
roaming spills. Clear the maze for bonus. Original art + layout.

### 6. Traffic Buster — `fixed-shooter`
A bottom-lane defender clearing an incoming wave of traffic cones/hazards.
Waves speed up; score per hazard cleared.

### 7. Cross the Interstate — `road-crosser`
Hop lane by lane across a busy interstate (and a river of… Big Gulps) to reach
the store. Each safe crossing scores; one hit ends the run.

### 8. Fuel Line — `snake`
Grow a fuel line by collecting pumps without crossing yourself. Classic snake,
Extra Mile skin.

### 9. Night Shift Defender — `space-shooter`
Defend the store from a descending wave through the night shift. Original
fixed/descending shooter.

## Thursday

### 10. The Extra Mile Racer — `car-racing` **(head-to-head)**
A top-down/endless racer down the strip. **Two modes:** solo time-trial (chase
your own best) and **live head-to-head** (two players, same track, first to the
finish / best time). Score = lap time (lower is better). This is the one live
2-player game.

### 11. Mystery Challenge — `mystery-challenge`
A surprise single-day challenge "revealed on the day" — I'd build it as a quick
reaction/skill mini-game (e.g. a reflex "pump the gas at the right moment"
meter). Score = points.
**Content needed:** confirm you're happy with a reaction mini-game, or tell me
the specific challenge you have in mind.

## Friday
Repeats **The Extra Mile Racer** + the Wednesday arcade set. No new builds.
